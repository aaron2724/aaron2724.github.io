Making HR and payroll migration data reliable and actionable.

A project overview of data reconciliation, migration validation and exception reporting during a complex enterprise HR and payroll transition.

01. Project context

Enterprise HR and payroll migrations require data to move consistently between systems. Differences in data structures, business rules and source quality can create exceptions that need to be understood before and after a transition.

This public case study is a high-level account of working methods. Internal datasets, employee information, detailed system configurations and unpublished performance figures are intentionally excluded.

02. The challenge

Reconcile source and target datasets, investigate discrepancies and present findings in a form that project stakeholders could use to prioritise remediation and validate migration readiness.

03. My analytical approach

  • Structured source extracts into comparable datasets, with repeatable transformation steps.
  • Developed matching and comparison logic to identify data inconsistencies.
  • Used Power Query and Excel analysis to group, investigate and report exceptions.
  • Translated complex discrepancies into clear summaries for stakeholder review and follow-up.

04. Types of deliverables

  • Repeatable reconciliation and data-quality workflows.
  • Exception summaries to support investigation.
  • Migration validation and follow-up reporting.

Illustrative, anonymised workflow examples may be added after content review.

05. Outcomes and evidence

The work supported visibility of migration discrepancies and enabled more structured validation discussions. Quantified results and project-specific impact statements will be added only when they can be verified and cleared for public use.

Key capability demonstrated: turning complex migration datasets into traceable analysis and decision-ready reporting.
